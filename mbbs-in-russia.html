Is Russia right for you?

Figures marked To be confirmed are being checked with each university before we publish them. Ask a counsellor for this year's figures.

Indian students choose Russia for MBBS for its availability of seats, English-medium options and established student support. The right choice depends on your budget, goals and eligibility, and a Movira counsellor will compare it with other destinations for you.

Why students choose Russia

  • Large choice of state and federal medical universities
  • English-medium MBBS programmes at many universities
  • Strong clinical training in university-run hospitals
  • Well-developed Indian student communities and hostels

Usually a good fit if…

  • You want to become a doctor and are ready for several years of study abroad
  • Your family has planned the cost of the whole programme
  • You're willing to prepare for India's licensing exam from the first year

Think twice if…

  • The budget only covers the first year or two
  • The plan is to practise in India, but nobody has looked at the licensing exam yet
  • Your family isn't comfortable with several years of study far from home

Eligibility

  • Class 12 (or equivalent) with Physics, Chemistry and Biology
  • NEET requirement for Indian students who plan to practise in India (confirm the current rules on the NMC website)
  • Minimum age and marks as set by the university
  • A valid passport and the documents listed on our admission-process page

After MBBS: practising in India

To practise in India after an overseas MBBS, graduates must meet NMC rules, including the licensing exam: FMGE, or NExT once it applies to them. The rules change, so we help you plan for them from year one.

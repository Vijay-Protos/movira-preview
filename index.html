Eligibility

Are you ready to apply for MBBS abroad?

Check the basics before you start. Rules change, so we verify the current position with you.

  • Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology
  • NEET requirement for Indian students — confirm current NMC rules
  • A valid passport, or time to apply for one
  • Budget plan for the full programme, not only year one

FAQ

Need help? Find your answers here

Many choose it for the wider availability of seats, English-medium programmes and a predictable admission route. The right choice depends on your budget, goals and eligibility — a counsellor can help you compare.

Indian students who plan to practise in India are generally expected to meet NEET requirements. Rules are updated by the NMC, so please confirm the current position with us and on the NMC website before applying.

Recognition depends on the university and on meeting NMC conditions. We help you shortlist only universities you can verify, and we encourage you to check the NMC list yourself.

Most universities we guide students to teach MBBS in English, and many also teach the local language during the programme to help with patient interaction.

Costs vary by country and university and include tuition, hostel, food, insurance and travel. We give you an itemised, year-by-year breakdown for each shortlisted university in writing.

Timelines depend on the university and intake. Starting early — ideally several months before the intake — gives time for documents, offer letters and visa processing.

05Eligibility

Is MBBS abroad right for your child?

Most universities ask for the basics below. Rules for Indian students are set by the NMC and do change, so we check the current position with you and show you where to read it.

Eligibility self-check

Think twice if…

  • The budget only covers the first year or two
  • The plan is to practise in India, but nobody has looked at the licensing exam yet
  • Your family isn't comfortable with several years of study far from home

Check the NMC list before you pay anyone, including us.

Short guide

Study abroad documents checklist

A tidy file saves weeks. Use this checklist to get ready before you apply.

By the Movira counselling team

Identity and travel: a passport valid well beyond your intended stay, and passport-size photographs to the university's specification.

Academic records: mark sheets and certificates, with attested copies where required.

Financial documents: proof of funds and, where applicable, sponsor or loan documents.

Admission and visa papers: the offer letter or invitation, medical and police clearance if requested, and any translations or apostilles the destination demands.

Always check the exact list with the university and embassy — requirements differ by country and change over time.

Check it yourself

The exact list comes from the university and the destination's embassy or consulate. Check both official websites before you apply.
